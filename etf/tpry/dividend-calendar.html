📚 How the $TPRY Dividend Calendar Works

Ex-dividend date: the cutoff. Own $TPRY before this date to get that month's payout. Buy on or after it and the payout goes to the seller.

Pay date: when the cash lands in your account, usually a few days after the ex-date.

$TPRY has gone ex-dividend around the 27th of the month over the last year, so the next one should land around Dec 25, 2026. Funds announce the exact date shortly before, so treat this as a guide.
