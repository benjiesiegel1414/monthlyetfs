📚 What Total Return Tells You

Total return is the full result of owning $SVOL: the change in the share price plus every dividend, reinvested. It is the fairest way to compare an income fund with anything else.

A high yield with a weak total return means the price is giving back much of what the fund pays out. A total return that keeps up with the yield means the fund is paying you without eating into the price.

$SVOL Total Return Trend FAQ

What is $SVOL's total return?

+0.6% over the last year, counting the share price and reinvested dividends. Over 3 years it averaged +1.9% per year.

What is the difference between total return and price return?

Price return only counts the share price. Total return also counts the dividends. For $SVOL, dividends added about +18.9% on top of the price move over the last year.

What was $SVOL's worst drop?
