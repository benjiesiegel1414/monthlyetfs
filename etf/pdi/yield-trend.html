📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $PDI or unusually high or low.

Listed yield18.0%The yield shown across MonthlyETFs, based on the fund's reported figure.
Trailing 12-month yield18.9%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate18.9%The latest monthly payout times 12, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$PDI Yield Trend FAQ

What is $PDI's yield right now?

$PDI's trailing 12-month yield is 18.9% at a share price of $14.01 (Nov 25, 2026). Its listed yield on MonthlyETFs is 18.0%.

Is $PDI's yield going up or down?

It was 14.8% a year ago and is 18.9% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed +0.0% and its share price changed -21.9%.

What is the highest yield $PDI has had?

25.5% on Dec 18, 2015, when the share price was $27.25. Its lowest was 7.9% on Sep 14, 2018.

Why are there different yield numbers for $PDI?

The listed yield uses the fund's reported figure, the trailing yield uses what it actually paid over 12 months, and the distribution rate uses only the latest payout times 12. They usually land close together, but they split apart when payouts or the price move quickly.
