📚 How This Calculator Works

We take the amount you invest, divide by $PDI's latest closing price to get your shares, then multiply by the latest monthly payout of $0.221 per share. The 12-month average payout is $0.221, so the "At 12-Mo Avg" column shows a smoother estimate.

Keep in mind: monthly ETFs change their payouts, the share price moves every day, and taxes come out of what you receive in a regular brokerage account. Reinvesting the payouts instead of spending them buys more shares, which grows future income. See what that did over time on the $PDI growth of $10,000 page.
