📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $MAXI or unusually high or low.

Listed yield4.9%The yield shown across MonthlyETFs, based on the fund's reported figure.
Trailing 12-month yield19.4%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate4.8%The latest monthly payout times 12, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$MAXI Yield Trend FAQ

What is $MAXI's yield right now?

Is $MAXI's yield going up or down?

It was 51.8% a year ago and is 19.4% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed -82.1% and its share price changed -52.2%.
