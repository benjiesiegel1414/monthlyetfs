📚 Why Reinvesting Matters

The growth of $10,000 shows what one investment turned into. The gold line assumes every payout bought more $XLEI shares on the ex-date. The dashed line ignores the payouts and only tracks the share price.

For high income funds the price line often lags, because a big part of the return is paid out as cash. A fund whose price line keeps sinking can still have a decent gold line, but only if you reinvest. Check the $XLEI price decay check to see how the share price has held up.
