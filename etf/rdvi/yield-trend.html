📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $RDVI or unusually high or low.

Listed yield8.5%The yield shown across MonthlyETFs, based on the fund's reported figure.
Trailing 12-month yield8.8%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate8.8%The latest monthly payout times 12, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

What is $RDVI's yield right now?

$RDVI's trailing 12-month yield is 8.8% at a share price of $26.68 (Nov 23, 2026). Its listed yield on MonthlyETFs is 8.5%.

Is $RDVI's yield going up or down?

It was 8.4% a year ago and is 8.8% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed +10.2% and its share price changed +6.1%.

Why are there different yield numbers for $RDVI?
