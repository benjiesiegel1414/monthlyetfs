📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $QVOL or unusually high or low.

Listed yield13.1%The yield shown across MonthlyETFs, based on the fund's reported figure.
Trailing 12-month yieldn/aWhat the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate16.9%The latest monthly payout times 12, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$QVOL Yield Trend FAQ

What is $QVOL's yield right now?

Why are there different yield numbers for $QVOL?
