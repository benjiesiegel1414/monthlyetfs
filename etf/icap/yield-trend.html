📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $ICAP or unusually high or low.

Listed yield11.2%The yield shown across MonthlyETFs, based on the fund's reported figure.
Trailing 12-month yield12.2%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate12.6%The latest monthly payout times 12, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$ICAP Yield Trend FAQ

What is $ICAP's yield right now?

$ICAP's trailing 12-month yield is 12.2% at a share price of $24.30 (Nov 17, 2026). Its listed yield on MonthlyETFs is 11.2%.

Is $ICAP's yield going up or down?

It was 8.7% a year ago and is 12.2% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed +26.3% and its share price changed -9.7%.

Why are there different yield numbers for $ICAP?
