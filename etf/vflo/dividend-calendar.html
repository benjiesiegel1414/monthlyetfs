📚 How the $VFLO Dividend Calendar Works

Ex-dividend date: the cutoff. Own $VFLO before this date to get that month's payout. Buy on or after it and the payout goes to the seller.

Pay date: when the cash lands in your account, usually a few days after the ex-date.

$VFLO has gone ex-dividend around the 9th of the month over the last year, so the next one should land around Dec 9, 2026. Funds announce the exact date shortly before, so treat this as a guide.
