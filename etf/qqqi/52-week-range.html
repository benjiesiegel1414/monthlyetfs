📚 What the 52-Week Range Means

The 52-week range is the lowest and highest closing price over the last year. Where today's price falls inside it is a quick read on momentum: near the high means the price has been holding up, near the low means it has been sliding.

For monthly income ETFs, the price drops by about the payout on every ex-date, so the range alone does not show how you did. Pair it with the $QQQI total return trend.

Does the 52-week range include dividends?

No. It only tracks the share price. Monthly ETFs drop by about the payout amount on each ex-date, so high payers often sit lower in their range than their total return suggests.
