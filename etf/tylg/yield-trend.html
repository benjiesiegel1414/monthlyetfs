📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $TYLG or unusually high or low.

Listed yield11.8%The yield shown across MonthlyETFs, based on the fund's reported figure.
Trailing 12-month yield10.4%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate11.4%The latest monthly payout times 12, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$TYLG Yield Trend FAQ

What is $TYLG's yield right now?

$TYLG's trailing 12-month yield is 10.4% at a share price of $36.26 (Nov 27, 2026). Its listed yield on MonthlyETFs is 11.8%.

Is $TYLG's yield going up or down?

It was 7.8% a year ago and is 10.4% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed +36.3% and its share price changed +1.5%.
