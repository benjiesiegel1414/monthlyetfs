📚 Reading $CSHI's Dividend History

The dividend history lists every cash payout $CSHI has made per share, by ex-dividend date. You own the payout if you hold shares before the ex-date.

Many monthly ETFs, especially option income funds, set each payout from what the fund earned that month, so the amount moves around. Look at the trend more than any single month: a steady or rising line is a good sign, a line that keeps stepping down usually means less income ahead.
