📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $CSHI or unusually high or low.

Listed yield5.0%The yield shown across MonthlyETFs, based on the fund's reported figure.
Trailing 12-month yield5.3%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate5.0%The latest monthly payout times 12, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$CSHI Yield Trend FAQ

What is $CSHI's yield right now?

$CSHI's trailing 12-month yield is 5.3% at a share price of $49.33 (Nov 25, 2026). Its listed yield on MonthlyETFs is 5.0%.

Is $CSHI's yield going up or down?

It was 4.7% a year ago and is 5.3% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed +9.8% and its share price changed -1.1%.

Why are there different yield numbers for $CSHI?
