📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $DGRW or unusually high or low.

Listed yield2.1%The yield shown across MonthlyETFs, based on the fund's reported figure.
Trailing 12-month yield1.3%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate0.9%The latest monthly payout times 12, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$DGRW Yield Trend FAQ

What is $DGRW's yield right now?

$DGRW's trailing 12-month yield is 1.3% at a share price of $92.29 (Nov 20, 2026). Its listed yield on MonthlyETFs is 2.1%.

Is $DGRW's yield going up or down?

It was 1.4% a year ago and is 1.3% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed -2.1% and its share price changed +6.3%.

Why are there different yield numbers for $DGRW?
