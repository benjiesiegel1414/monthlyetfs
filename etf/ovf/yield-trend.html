📚 What Does Yield Trend Mean?

A fund's yield is how much it pays you each year as a percent of its share price. The yield trend shows how that percent has moved over time, so you can tell if today's yield is normal for $OVF or unusually high or low.

Listed yield10.6%The yield shown across MonthlyETFs, based on the fund's reported figure.
Trailing 12-month yield14.0%What the fund actually paid over the last 12 months, divided by today's price.
Distribution rate11.8%The latest monthly payout times 12, divided by today's price. Reacts fastest to changes.

How to read it: a steady yield trend means the payouts and the price are moving together. A yield that climbs while the price falls can be a warning sign, because the payout may get cut later. A yield that climbs while the price holds up usually means the fund is paying more.

$OVF Yield Trend FAQ

What is $OVF's yield right now?

$OVF's trailing 12-month yield is 14.0% at a share price of $26.67 (Nov 20, 2026). Its listed yield on MonthlyETFs is 10.6%.

Is $OVF's yield going up or down?

It was 4.8% a year ago and is 14.0% now. Over that year its payouts per share changed +177.3% and its share price changed -4.7%.

Why are there different yield numbers for $OVF?
