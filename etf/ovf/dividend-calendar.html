📚 How the $OVF Dividend Calendar Works

Ex-dividend date: the cutoff. Own $OVF before this date to get that month's payout. Buy on or after it and the payout goes to the seller.

Pay date: when the cash lands in your account, usually a few days after the ex-date.

$OVF has gone ex-dividend around the 27th of the month over the last year, so the next one should land around Nov 27, 2026. Funds announce the exact date shortly before, so treat this as a guide.
